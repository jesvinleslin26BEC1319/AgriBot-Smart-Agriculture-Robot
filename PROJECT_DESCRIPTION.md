# AgriBot – Smart Agriculture Rover

## Problem
Farmers often need to manually monitor soil moisture and crop conditions.
This can require significant time and can result in unnecessary water usage.

## Proposed Solution
AgriBot is an autonomous agricultural rover designed to monitor soil
conditions and provide targeted irrigation.

## Features
- Soil moisture monitoring
- Automatic irrigation
- Temperature and humidity monitoring
- Obstacle detection
- IoT-based monitoring
- Future AI-based plant disease detection

## Hardware
- ESP32 / Arduino
- Soil moisture sensor
- DHT11/DHT22 sensor
- Ultrasonic sensor
- Motor driver
- DC motors
- Water pump
- Camera
- Battery

## Working
The rover moves through the agricultural field and measures soil moisture.
When the soil becomes too dry, the controller activates the water pump.
Ultrasonic sensors help the rover avoid obstacles.

## Future Improvements
- GPS navigation
- Solar charging
- AI crop disease detection
- Mobile application
- Automated field mapping

## Goal
To develop an affordable robotic system that can reduce water wastage
and simplify agricultural monitoring.
